Alphabet．2026 Q3 Earnings, Unpacked

Cloud revenue doubles while FCF stays negative for a second quarter; investment losses depress headline EPS.
2026-07-01 ~ 2026-09-30Released:2026-10-282026-10-31Draft complete · Unpublished

TL;DR

  • Revenue of $127.577B rose 24.7% YoY and 6.5% QoQ. Operating income of $40.576B rose 29.9% YoY but fell 0.5% QoQ.
  • Gross margin fell to 59.7% from 61.7%; operating margin was 31.8%. Faster revenue did not translate into group margin expansion.
  • GAAP EPS was $2.12. The disclosed equity-loss impact was $0.55 per share; adding only that item gives a research non-GAAP $2.67, not a comprehensive company core EPS.
  • Quarterly FCF deteriorated to −$9.065B from −$5.855B; OCF of $42.290B lagged PP&E spending of $51.355B.
