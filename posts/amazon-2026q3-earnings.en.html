Amazon．2026 Q3 Earnings, Unpacked

AWS accelerates while cash recovery trails investment. Draft complete · Unpublished.
2026-07-01 ~ 2026-09-30Released:2026-10-292026-10-31

TL;DR

AWS growth reaches 39.3%, but quarterly free cash flow is about −$33.5B: operating profit and cash recovery diverge.

  • AWS revenue was $46.0B, up 39.3% YoY versus about 37% in Q2. AWS operating margin was 38.7%, down from 39.4% in Q2. Growth accelerated while margin declined.
  • Group operating income of $28.076B exceeded the prior $26.5B guidance ceiling. Revenue of $201.944B was close to the $202B upper bound.
  • Quarterly operating cash flow of $42.422B minus net cash capex of $75.878B implies FCF of −$33.456B. The −$42.4B trailing figure is a different period.
  • GAAP EPS was $1.89 versus $1.95 a year earlier. The prior year included $9.5B of pretax Anthropic investment gains; the headline decline does not establish an operating deterioration.

Segments and revenue quality

North America: $112.9B revenue (+6%) and $8.7B operating income. International: $43.1B revenue (+5%, +8% currency-neutral) and $1.6B operating income. AWS: $46.0B revenue (+39.3%) and $17.8B operating income, about 22.8% of group revenue but 63.4% of operating income. Prime Day moved to Q2 in most major markets; management estimates that this reduced Q3 YoY growth by about four percentage points. That is an estimate, not restated revenue.

Guidance

Q4 revenue guidance is $240–247B (+12–16% YoY), with operating income of $28.5–33.5B. Full-year cash capex remains about $220B. Currency-neutral advertising growth was 12% in Q3; management expects high-teens growth in Q4. These are management forecasts. No previous guidance for the same Q4 period was verified, so a new quarterly range is not labeled a raise.

Prior watch items revisited

  • Confirmed: AWS growth accelerated from about 37% to 39.3%, its sixth consecutive acceleration. Relative peer growth remains unverified on a comparable basis.
  • Unresolved: cash capex remains $220B, with no new increase this quarter. Whether this is the final annual revision requires the year to finish.
  • Not met: TTM FCF did not recover to positive territory; it deteriorated from about −$7.6B in Q2 to −$42.4B.

Risks and counterarguments

AI equipment consumes cash before subsequent depreciation hits profit. A high current operating margin does not establish an adequate cash return on expansion. AWS margin declined sequentially; memory, energy and capacity costs remain risks. Prime Day timing, FX and holiday demand complicate retail growth comparisons.

Next-quarter watch list

  1. Does Q4 AWS revenue grow at least 39.3% YoY while operating margin stays at or above 38.7%?
  2. Do Q4 revenue and operating income reach the $240B and $28.5B lower guidance bounds?
  3. Does annual cash capex remain about $220B, and does TTM FCF improve from −$42.4B?
