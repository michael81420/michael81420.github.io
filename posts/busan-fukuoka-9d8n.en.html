[Day 1] Arrival → Nampo-dong & the Songdo coast

Arrival and hotel (06:00–12:00)

  • 06:00 Flight out, 09:20 land in Busan.
  • 11:00 Subway to Brown Suites Busan Station to drop off luggage. Take exit 6 of Busan Station subway, walk to exit 10 on the 2F of Busan Train Station, then the footbridge leads straight to the check-in desk.
  • 12:00 Head to Jagalchi, arriving 12:30.

Jagalchi Market (12:30–14:00)

  • Why go: Korea's largest seafood market — pick your fish downstairs, have it prepared upstairs.

Songdo Marine Cable Car and coastline (14:00–16:10)

  • Getting there: Taxi from Jagalchi to the Songdo Bay cable car station, 10–15 min.
  • Songdo Marine Cable Car pass: Crosses Songdo Bay; the crystal cabins have glass floors over open water.
  • Songdo Skywalk / Yonggung Cloud Bridge: Boardwalks running out over the sea to a rocky islet.
  • Sky Harbor observatory: At the hilltop station, overlooking the whole Songdo shoreline.

Free time and dinner (16:10~)

  • Bupyeong Kkangtong Market: The old "canned goods" night market — street food and imported snacks.
  • Busan Tower (Yongdusan Park) pass, Lotte Department Store.
  • Nampo-dong: Lee Jae Mo Pizza, Baekhwa grilled intestines, BIFF Square.
  • Optional: 14:00 Yeongdo Bridge lift show — the deck is raised once a day.

[Day 2] Haedong Yonggungsa → a full day in Haeundae

Haedong Yonggungsa (10:00–11:30)

  • Getting there: Up at 08:30 for breakfast, taxi from the hotel at 10:00.
  • Why go: A rare seaside temple built onto the cliffs, with waves breaking below the stairs — the most photogenic spot in Busan.

Lotte Premium Outlet Dongbusan lunch (11:30–13:00)

  • About 5 minutes from the temple — lunch and shopping on the way through.

Skyline Luge Busan (13:00–15:00) pass

  • Why go: Chairlift up, gravity cart down, with sea views the whole way. Buy several runs.

Haeundae and The Bay 101 (15:30–19:00)

  • 15:30 Haeundae beach market (weekends only); the giant ox-bone soup place near The Bay 101 is nearby.
  • 17:00 BUSAN X the SKY (100F observatory) pass, then The Bay 101 for the classic reflection shot of the Marine City skyline.

Dinner: Obanjang BBQ (19:00)

  • Korean BBQ near Haeundae to close out the day.

[Day 3] Cheongsapo Sky Capsule → Gwangalli

Cheongsapo Skywalk area (10:00–11:30)

  • Getting there: Taxi from the hotel at 10:00.
  • Why go: A glass-floored walkway over the sea beside the red-and-white lighthouse pair; brunch on bread at diart coffee next door.

Sky Capsule (11:30–12:30)

  • Route: Cheongsapo → Mipo, in retro four-seat pods that crawl along the coastal rail line.
  • Booking: Reserve on the official site in advance — popular slots sell out.

Haeundae Traditional Market (12:30–14:30)

  • Lunch on market street food, then walk back into central Haeundae.

Shinsegae Spa Land (14:30–17:00) pass

  • Why go: The big jjimjilbang inside Centum City — 22 spring-water baths and a range of sauna rooms. The best recovery stop of the trip.

Gwangalli Beach and bars (17:00–)

  • 17:00 Gwangalli Beach for the Gwangan Bridge light-up.
  • 19:00 Beachfront bars.

[Day 4] Free day in Busan → ferry check-in

The sheet marks this day "for reference only" — everyone plans their own morning, then the group meets before boarding.

Options after check-out (10:00–15:30)

  • Oryukdo Skywalk: A cantilevered glass walkway straight out over the water.
  • Gamcheon Culture Village: The painted hillside village, Busan's most famous walk.
  • Taejongdae: Sea-cliff park on the south end of Yeongdo, with a loop tram.
  • Seomyeon: The main shopping district; also casinos and clinics.

Meeting up and boarding (15:30–18:00)

  • 15:30 Meet for a meal, collect luggage from the hotel.
  • 18:00 ferry check-in — overnight on board, arriving in Hakata at 08:00.

[Day 5] Arrive in Hakata → Nokonoshima → Dazaifu

Arrival and car pick-up (08:00–09:30)

  • 08:00 Ferry docks at Hakata Port.
  • 09:00 Store luggage at Hakata Station and pick up the rental car (09:00).
  • 09:30 Drive to Meinohama ferry terminal.

Nokonoshima Island Park (10:15–14:10)

  • Outbound: 10:15 ferry (arrive 10:25) → 10:35 bus to the park (arrive 10:48).
  • Return: 12:33 or 13:15 bus back to the port → 13:00 or 14:00 ferry (back in Hakata 14:10).
  • Why go: A small island in Hakata Bay with seasonal flower fields over the sea — cosmos in autumn.

Lunch in Hakata and Dazaifu Tenmangu (14:10–18:30)

  • Lunch: Motsunabe Rakutenchi, Tenjin main store — Fukuoka's signature offal hotpot.
  • Getting there: Leave Hakata at 15:30, about 30 minutes by car, arriving 16:00.
  • Why go: The shrine to Sugawara no Michizane, god of learning. Don't miss the umegae-mochi stalls and the Kengo Kuma-designed Starbucks on the approach.
  • 18:00 Leave Dazaifu, 18:30 back in Hakata.

Dinner: Yakuin yakiniku (19:00) reserved

  • Booked via TableCheck.

[Day 6] Ukiha Inari Shrine → Yufuin → Beppu

Ukiha Inari Shrine (09:00–10:30)

  • Getting there: Leave Hakata at 09:00, about an hour by car.
  • Why go: 91 red torii climbing the hillside, with the Chikugo plain spread out below from the top.

Yufuin (11:30–14:30)

  • Getting there: Leave Ukiha at 10:30, about an hour.
  • Yunotsubo street: The dense main shopping street, ending at Lake Kinrin.
  • Lake Kinrin, Tenso Shrine: Hot-spring steam over the lake and a torii standing in the water.
Food
  • Yufu Kamameshi "Kokoro": Pot-cooked rice made to order.
  • B-speak: The town's famous roll cake — often sold out by early afternoon.
  • Tenjosajiki: An old-house café by Lake Kinrin.

Beppu Suginoi Hotel (15:00–)

  • Getting there: Leave Yufuin at 14:30, about 40 minutes via the Kasumi-Kirin lookout.
  • Stay: Beppu SUGINOI HOTEL, dinner and breakfast included.
  • Why go: The terraced Tanayu open-air baths look straight down over Beppu Bay. Everything after 15:00 is free time.

[Day 7] Beppu onsen → Kokura and the Kitakyushu arcades

Check-out and a local bath (11:00–13:00)

  • 11:00 Check out, 11:30 soak at a neighbourhood public bath — Beppu's everyday onsen.
  • 13:00 Drive to Kokura.

Kokura Castle and Tanga Market (13:00–17:00)

  • Kokura Castle: Built by Hosokawa Tadaoki; the keep is open, with a Japanese garden alongside.
  • 14:30 Tanga Market: "Kitakyushu's kitchen" — a century-old market known for unagi and prepared food.

Arcades and dinner

  • Riverwalk Kitakyushu, the Chuo shopping street and Uomachi Ginten-gai — Japan's first covered shopping arcade, running through to Tanga Market.
  • Dinner: Unagi Inakaan, Kokura's best-known eel restaurant.
  • 20:00 Drive back to Fukuoka, staying again at Riverside Inn Hakata.

[Day 8] A day along the Itoshima coast

Sakurai Futamigaura and Sakurai Shrine (09:30–13:00)

  • Getting there: Leave Fukuoka at 09:30, about 40 minutes.
  • Meoto Iwa: The white torii in the sea with the "married rocks" behind it — Itoshima's signature view.
  • Lunch: Itoshima Kaisendo (private room, reserved for ~11:00).

Keya no Oto + boat (13:20–15:20)

  • Why go: One of Japan's three great basalt sea caves; you need the sightseeing boat to get inside (cancelled in rough seas).
  • 15:20 Back to Fukuoka, 16:20 return the car.

Dinner: Daimyo Hete 2 (18:30) reserved

  • In the Daimyo district, booked through tabelog (booking fee applies).

[Day 9] Last shopping in Hakata → home

Meeting up and departure

  • Free time during the day, 16:30 meet at Hakata Station.
  • 18:30 Leave for Fukuoka Airport — about 6 minutes by subway, one of the closest airports to a city centre in Japan.
  • 19:00 At the airport, 21:00 take off.
