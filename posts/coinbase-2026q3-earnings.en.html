Coinbase．2026 Q3 Earnings, Unpacked

Core profit recovers and share remains above 10%; most headline net income comes from investments.
2026-07-01 ~ 2026-09-30Released:2026-10-292026-10-31Draft complete · Unpublished

TL;DR

  • Revenue of $1.2156B fell 35.0% YoY and 0.4% QoQ; net transaction revenue of $590.2M fell 1% sequentially.
  • Adjusted EBITDA was $333M (+60% QoQ), with adjusted net income of $77.7M. GAAP net income of $718.7M included crypto and investment gains.
  • Subscription/services revenue of $570.2M rose 3%, comprised 49% of net revenue and landed inside prior $500–580M guidance.
  • Crypto trading share reached 10.7%, the fourth quarterly gain. Prediction-market annualized revenue exceeded $200M; this is a run rate, not quarterly booked revenue.

Segments

Revenue comprises $590.2M net transactions, $570.2M subscriptions/services and $55.1M corporate interest/other. Average USDC held was $22B (+49%). A rising subscription mix partly reflects weak transaction revenue.

Guidance

Q4 subscription/services guidance is $550–630M; adjusted expenses $1.007–1.157B. Annual adjusted expenses narrow to $4.15–4.30B. Transaction revenue of about $165M through October 25 is not full-quarter guidance.

Prior watch items revisited

  • Subscription/services revenue of $570.2M was within $500–580M.
  • Adjusted EBITDA recovered from $207.8M to about $333M.
  • Trading share of 10.7% stayed above 10% and the prior 10.3%.

The official deck was obtained; complete Q&A was not verified. Management’s resilience narrative does not establish independence from crypto cycles.

Valuation

Risks
