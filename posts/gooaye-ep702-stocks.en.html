7. Assessment

What holds up

His read of the rumor is restrained. He calls it a rumor, focuses on 3.2T, says near-term impact is limited, and points out that what’s really blocked is China’s in-house laser/DSP path — consistent with 404K and cnyes. “Policy direction → market trades early” matches the 10/2 tape, and Taiwan optics revenue really is strong (Gloria +152%, Lian Jun +110%, Ultra Source +106%).

Discount this

Taiwan reshoring is indirect imagination — he labels it that way himself, but the market already priced it with limit-ups. He holds optics, so his bullish framing (“won’t move fast, but worth betting early”) has a stake behind it. The MediaTek AI-budget details have no public source. Passives/power semis “finally moving” is a bounce from −29% to −51% below highs, and Advanced Power’s August revenue was still −3%.

Risks

If the curb becomes final it may speed up Chinese in-house development and capacity, sparking a price war; upstream materials are Chinese, so retaliation is a risk. Valuations are stretched: LITE’s PS is at the 99.2nd percentile, MRVL 80x forward, MXL 117x forward; Taiwan optics trade mostly at 50–250x trailing. The biggest 1-month movers — MXL (+78%), SMTC (+44%), Unice (+41%) — are small momentum names with high give-back risk on a rumor rally.
