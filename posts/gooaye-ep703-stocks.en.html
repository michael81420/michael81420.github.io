8. Assessment

What holds up

The facts are almost all right. +72% YTD, fourth-largest market cap, July’s −16.4% and futures contango (+173 pts Oct, +596 pts Dec) all check out. “The index is at highs but many names aren’t” is supported by the data, and his response is rules-based position management, not a top call.

Take with a grain of salt

“TSMC EPS says 50,000 isn’t the top” gives no multiple or EPS, so it can’t be falsified; TSMC itself lags the index YTD and its ADR valuation is at the 87th percentile, so part of the support is multiple expansion. “Shortages hurt” isn’t uniform either: Compal and Inventec margins fell, but ASUS went from 14.9% to 17.2% — check whether margins are really squeezed before picking a volume play.

Risks

The volume hedge and the core book share Taiwan beta and fall together in a systemic pullback. Memory revenue is still +257% to +577% YoY, far from the “supply eases” inflection, so volume plays may wait a long time. Packaging and thermal are also in the shortage chain yet at highs, so “shortage names are the losers” can’t be applied blanket-wide.
