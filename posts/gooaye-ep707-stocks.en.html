1. What this episode covers

About 70% is chat (games, running, drinks for nurses, family, European migration, watches). The investing part runs: SpaceX earnings call, NAND demand warning, positioning, substrates, CCL and clean rooms, then sentiment and positioning.

The transcript is speech-to-text with many homophone errors; suspected garble such as "BADC" is not used here.

2. Substrates: strong fundamentals, flat shares

The host offers three reasons: capital crowding (too many names with 50%+ revenue growth, limited money, so each waits its turn), clean-room tool delays, and poor yield. He treats online cranks bashing substrates as a sign of unstable positioning, and says he leans toward the pricier, better-chart name over the cheaper one. "Cracking" does not hold up: ITEQ is +63% over 20 days and only 3.9% off its high.

On yield, he cites EMIB substrates at 50–60% and Samsung at 20–30%. TrendForce (9/23) puts EMIB near 45% (about 30% in 2Q26) with targets of 50% in 4Q26 and 60% in 1Q27; the Samsung figure could not be found.

3. SpaceX and compute

The host relays Musk: no long compute contracts, only short ones, and compute investment pays back in 6 months. His read is that long contracts usually signal a near-top, so avoiding them means demand is still rising, and double-digit financing rates are no issue.

Counterpoint: not signing long contracts may simply let the seller keep pricing power, a price risk for buyers; the 6-month payback has no independent source, and last quarter the public line was under a year. FQ3 GAAP net income of $4.40B includes about $1.0B of non-operating income and tax gains; operating income of $2.79B is the cleaner base. The prior print (8/5) fell 13.6%.

4. Peer scan: AI substrates, high-end PCB and CCL

Group definition: ABF substrates, high-end PCB and CCL, driven by large AI-accelerator packages and EMIB/CoWoS-style advanced packaging, plus the upstream capex bottleneck (clean rooms and facilities). Cycle intensity is a deep commodity cycle plus AI shortage, so trailing PE is not a buy signal.
