1. Leadership: the event and the scenarios

✅ Confirmed: NVIDIA announced on November 12 that Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and director, with Colette Kress serving as interim CEO. Its 8-K records the board accepting the resignation on November 11. A permanent successor is being sought; Tim Cook has not been announced as that successor.

The host sees Huang as a coordinator of capital, suppliers and aggressive specifications. His concern is that a successor focused on standardization and purchasing discipline could compress exceptional supplier profits. That is a transmission hypothesis, not proof of delays or margin losses.

❓ Unverified: a return to NVIDIA, a new venture, and appointment of Cook or another procurement specialist. Private allegations are excluded. Personal judgments are not operating evidence.

The counterargument is institutional continuity: engineering teams, product plans and customer commitments extend beyond one executive. Management says execution will continue; deliveries and guidance must test that commitment. A short-term relief rally can coexist with a higher long-term risk premium.

2. Specification upgrades and supplier premiums

Cycle first: AI compute is driven by structural adoption and platform upgrades, while individual components also face shortages, capacity additions and bargaining cycles. Growing end demand does not guarantee rising supplier margins.

The host uses Vera Rubin liquid cooling to illustrate the pace of specification upgrades. ✅ NVIDIA technical documentation supports liquid cooling and MGX racks capable of full liquid cooling. This does not establish any supplier's share, pricing or margins, or prove the counterfactual claim that every competitor needs NVIDIA to lead.

❓ Below 10x forward earnings: the host supplies no reproducible forecast period, core EPS, tax rate or diluted share count. At the Futu last price of $162.85, a multiple below 10x requires annual EPS above $16.285. This is an arithmetic threshold, not our earnings forecast. Trailing PE and broker targets do not resolve the missing denominator.

Two variables matter: sustainable core earnings and the multiple investors accept. Lower forecast earnings can erase an apparently cheap multiple. Buybacks are a capital-allocation possibility, not a guaranteed price floor or competitive advantage.

4. Three scenarios and their evidence gates

Expand a scenario to see what could change the conclusion. No probabilities are assigned and none is assumed to have occurred.

A: The team maintains the roadmap

Check deliveries, customer deployments and guidance. Stable execution would weaken the claim that leadership change necessarily slows technology; the risk premium could still rise.

B: Standardization and price pressure accelerate

Look for changes in supplier margins, ASPs, qualifications and purchasing terms. A procurement specialist's appointment alone does not prove industry profit compression; higher volume may offset price pressure.

C: The founder starts again or returns

Wait for a formal announcement, then examine teams, funding, products, intellectual property and production capability. A new venture does not immediately create market share; a return does not guarantee restoration of the old multiple.

5. Orbital AI needs commercial evidence

The host discusses Kessel and space computing as alternatives to immediate GPU-versus-ASIC trading. ✅ NVIDIA has announced SpaceXAI's plan to use an optimized Vera Rubin NVL72 system for its first-generation Starmind satellite. That supports the cooperation direction, not every Kessel design claim, purchase volume or named Taiwan supplier order.

Power, thermal management, reliability, launches and commercial deployment remain relevant. An architecture tied to NVIDIA cannot simply be treated as a hedge against NVIDIA exposure. Primary evidence is insufficient to name Taiwan suppliers or forecast revenue, so no tickers are guessed.

7. What to watch next

  • A permanent appointment, operating responsibilities, roadmap and delivery guidance.
  • Disclosed new customers and revenue at AMD, Intel or ASIC vendors, beyond relative stock performance.
  • Supplier pricing and margins, separating volumes, upgrades and purchasing pressure.
  • Verifiable orbital deployments, procurement and revenue before including the theme in earnings forecasts.

Credibility: the leadership event and architecture are verifiable; succession effects and the low-multiple claim need more evidence. Reducing chip exposure is the host's trading choice. This article has not demonstrated that it outperforms holding and does not turn it into a trading recommendation.
