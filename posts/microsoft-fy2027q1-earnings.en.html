Microsoft．FY2027 Q1 Earnings, Unpacked

Azure accelerates to 48% and gross margin recovers; delayed capex is not lower investment demand.
2026-07-01 ~ 2026-09-30Released:2026-10-282026-10-31Draft complete · Unpublished

TL;DR

  • Revenue of $92.279B grew 18.8% YoY and 2.5% QoQ; operating income of $45.915B rose 13.1% QoQ.
  • Gross margin recovered to 67.8% from 67.2%, below 69.0% a year earlier; operating margin was 49.8%.
  • GAAP EPS was $4.92; company non-GAAP EPS excluding OpenAI was $4.89, up 18%. Headline 32% growth overstates core growth.
  • Quarterly OCF of $58.000B less cash PP&E of $39.367B implies FCF of $18.633B. Capex including leases was a separate $45.2B measure.

Segments

Recast segments: Agents and Infra $76.795B (+25%); Devices and Consumer $15.484B (−3%). Do not compare directly with the former three segments. Commercial RPO was $677B (+73%); about 30% is expected within 12 months.

Guidance

FY27 Q2 revenue guidance is $96–97.1B, with Azure growth of 49–50% in constant currency. Q2 capex exceeds $50B; calendar 2026 capex is about $170B. Third-party capacity shifted to the fiscal second half; management still expects positive FY27 FCF.

Prior watch items revisited

  • Azure growth of 48% exceeded the prior approximately 45% CC outlook.
  • Gross margin recovered from 67.2% to 67.8%, though still down YoY.
  • Capex is quantified at $45.2B, below the prior roughly $50B expectation partly because deliveries slipped; investment demand is not disproved.

Conference call

[Call] Official remarks attribute Azure variability to third-party deliveries, CPU/GPU efficiencies and revenue per token. Acceleration is not solely new datacenters. Q&A was not coded question by question, so no frequency claims are made. Supply constraints remain in the outlook.

Valuation

Primarily structural growth, with enterprise and investment cycles. A raw-statement forward core EPS model and verified current price were not completed; forward PE is —. Broker targets, consensus forecasts and trailing PE are not valuation substitutes.

Risks

AI equipment and lease commitments separate cash, capex and depreciation timing; deferred spending may cluster later. Segment changes and OpenAI investments complicate comparisons.

Next-quarter watch list

  1. Does Q2 Azure constant-currency growth reach at least 49%?
  2. Does Q2 revenue reach $96B and cloud gross margin approach 65% guidance?
  3. Does Q2 capex exceed $50B while FCF remains positive?
