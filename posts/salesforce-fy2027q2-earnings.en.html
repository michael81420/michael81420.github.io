TL;DR · What actually mattered this quarter

💰 The headline EPS looks great, but half of it is an investment markup, not the core business. Non-GAAP EPS of $5.90 (+103% YoY) included $2.53 from a one-time unrealized gain on Salesforce's stake in AI startup Anthropic (the investment booked a $2.613B gain this quarter). Strip that out and core operating EPS was roughly $3.37 (our own calculation, $5.90 − $2.53; Salesforce does not report this figure — the release only discloses the gain's impact of $2.43 on GAAP EPS and $2.53 on non-GAAP EPS) — only about a 3% beat versus the $3.27 consensus, far less impressive than the headline number suggests.

📈 Bookings momentum is the real story this quarter, and it's solid. cRPO (bookings expected to be recognized over the next 12 months) came in at $33.5B, +14% YoY — the fastest growth in recent quarters. Total RPO was $66.3B, +11% YoY. About 3 points of the +11% revenue growth came from the Informatica acquisition ($456M); organic growth excluding the deal was roughly +8% — not fast, but steady.

🏦 Buybacks paused, replaced with debt-funded repurchases — the capital allocation logic has changed. Share repurchases this quarter were $0; instead, Salesforce is running a $25B accelerated share repurchase (ASR) funded by debt issuance. Management maintained FY27 operating/free cash flow growth guidance at roughly +4%–5% YoY. Using leverage to fund shareholder returns is a different logic than buying back stock from organic cash.

★ On valuation: the stock jumped more than 20% the day after earnings — the second-best day in company history — pushing forward PE to 15.95x (PEG 0.95). That's not expensive, but remember: a real chunk of the rally's justification was an accounting gain, not pure business acceleration.

3. Peer group scan: enterprise SaaS + AI agent monetization

Group definition: cloud application software (SaaS) sold to enterprises by seat/subscription, all pitching "AI agents that do the work" — CRM, ERP, HR/finance and creative tools each cover a slice, with ORCL/MSFT also running their own cloud infrastructure.

4. Watch next quarter

  • Whether core operating EPS (ex one-offs) can hit the Q3 guide of $3.42–3.44 — if it again leans on investment gains, the core business still isn't accelerating.
  • Whether cRPO YoY growth holds near +14%; a drop back to the +11%–12% range would mark this quarter's bookings strength as one-time rather than a trend.
  • Whether the Informatica contribution fades as the comp base rises — watch if organic revenue growth (ex-acquisition) holds around +8%.

5. Bull vs. bear

Bull case
cRPO +14% is the fastest in recent quarters — real booking strength; non-GAAP operating margin steady around 34%; forward PE of 15.95x and PEG of 0.95 aren't expensive.
Bear case
Nearly half the headline EPS beauty is an investment markup — the real beat is only +3%; buybacks paused in favor of debt-funded repurchases, changing the capital structure; organic revenue growth is only around +8%.
