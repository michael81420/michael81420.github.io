TL;DR · What actually mattered this quarter

📊 Revenue and EPS both beat, and subscription growth accelerated rather than slowed. Total revenue was $3.987B, +24% YoY (+22.5% CC); subscription revenue was $3.877B, +24.5% YoY — faster than last quarter's +22% and last year's +22.5%, a rare acceleration signal. Non-GAAP EPS was $0.90 (split-adjusted, vs. $0.81 a year ago), beating the $0.86 consensus.

⚠️ But margin didn't follow the good revenue news — it actually fell from last quarter's high. Non-GAAP operating margin was 29.5% this quarter, down sharply from Q1's 32% and back to the same 29.5% as a year ago. Management attributed part of this to federal government business timing, but it means this quarter's revenue acceleration didn't come from operating leverage — it's scale expansion on its own, worth watching next quarter to see if it was just a blip.

🤖 The AI monetization story here is the most concrete in the peer group right now. ServiceNow's AI annual contract value (ACV) crossed $1B this quarter, ahead of pace for the $1.5B year-end target. cRPO (bookings expected to be recognized over the next 12 months) was $13.20B, +21% YoY — slightly down from last year's +24.5%, but the absolute scale keeps growing, suggesting AI features are converting into real bookings, not just narrative.

★ On valuation: forward PE sits at 29.93x (PEG 1.22), the richest in the peer group, with trailing PE even higher at 84.73x (reflecting a depressed recent EPS base). This valuation has already priced in "AI acceleration" — if margin doesn't recover next quarter, the premium gets re-examined.

3. Peer group scan: enterprise workflow/SaaS + AI agent monetization

Group definition: cloud application software (SaaS) sold to enterprises by seat/subscription, all pitching "AI agents that do the work" — workflow/ITSM, CRM, ERP and HR/finance each cover a slice, with MSFT/ORCL also running their own cloud infrastructure.

4. Watch next quarter

  • Whether non-GAAP operating margin recovers to the Q3 guide of 31% — falling below this quarter's 29.5% again would suggest the margin pressure is structural, not just federal timing.
  • Whether AI ACV keeps adding roughly $100M+ per quarter, staying on pace toward the $1.5B full-year target.
  • Whether cRPO YoY growth stabilizes rather than continuing to slow from +24.5% to +21% — otherwise booking momentum may be weaker than the headline suggests.
