ServiceNow．2026 Q3 Earnings, Unpacked

Adjusted margin recovers, but GAAP profit falls and cRPO growth still slows.
2026-07-01 ~ 2026-09-30Released:2026-10-282026-10-31Draft complete · Unpublished

TL;DR

  • Revenue of $4.106B rose 20.5% YoY and 3.0% QoQ; subscription revenue was $3.988B (+21%).
  • Non-GAAP operating margin recovered to 32.5% from 29.5%; GAAP operating income of $385M was below $572M a year earlier.
  • Non-GAAP EPS was $1.00 versus GAAP $0.27; $633M SBC and $198M intangible amortization materially explain the operating-profit gap.
  • Company adjusted FCF was $822M. OCF of $839M minus $91M capex gives $748M before a $74M acquisition-cost addback.
