TL;DR · What actually mattered this quarter

📉 A record quarter that still fell short — the AI-memory narrative is now outrunning actual execution. Revenue of ₩79.32T (+257% YoY, +51% QoQ) and operating profit of ₩60.54T (+557% YoY, +61% QoQ) both hit all-time highs, but analysts had modeled roughly ₩84T revenue and ₩64T operating profit — actuals came in about 5% short on both, the first "record yet still a miss" quarter of this supercycle. The stock fell 9.6% on the day.

💰 The headline 1,242% net income jump is mostly not core-business earnings. Of the ₩93.92T net income, ₩63.3T came from a one-off investment gain tied to the Kioxia stake sale (plus ₩1.15T of FX gains) — a non-operating item larger than the ₩60.54T operating profit itself. The company did not disclose a clean core net income/EPS figure excluding this gain, and this note does not attempt to derive a precise one; the operating profit line is the clean measure to watch.

🚀 HBM4 began mass shipment this quarter with roughly 10 long-term customer agreements secured — that's the variable that actually decides the next few years. After the sell-off, forward PE is only around 4.5x — the lowest of the three major memory makers — with a PEG of 0.06. The reason it's cheap is clear: the market is digesting the fact that even SK hynix couldn't keep up with the expectations it helped create.

1. Where net income actually came from: the one-off is bigger than the core business

A record ₩60.54T operating profit is real and impressive. The problem is the headline net income figure contains a non-recurring gain bigger than that core business result. Without splitting it out, it's easy to overstate how strong this quarter's underlying profitability actually was.

2. Core numbers vs. the last two quarters (KRW trillion)

The company did not disclose a core net income/EPS figure excluding the one-off gain, and this note marks it "—" rather than deriving one. Use operating profit and operating margin to judge the core business. DRAM ASP rose roughly +30% QoQ this quarter and NAND ASP rose roughly mid-50% QoQ — the main driver of the core-business record.

3. Guidance: no dollar figures, just a roadmap

Unlike most US peers, SK hynix did not issue quantified Q3 or full-year revenue/margin guidance this quarter — only bit-shipment direction and a capex range. FY26 capex guidance was raised to the high-₩40T range (roughly ₩48–49T), while Q2 capex alone was only ₩10.67T — implying a significant acceleration in the second half, consistent with management's comments about ramping HBM4 production in H2.

4. Valuation: forward PE of 4.5x is the lowest in the group after the sell-off

Forward PE of roughly 4.5x (stockanalysis.com, as of 2026-09-24), trailing PE of 8.18x, PEG of just 0.06. Current price is about ₩1,862,000, market cap about ₩1,357.15T. Analyst consensus is Strong Buy (37 analysts, nearly all on buy), with an average target price of ₩3,184,025 — implying roughly +71% upside. Net cash is about ₩69.4T (₩88T cash minus ₩18.6T debt).

★ Context matters here: a 4.5x forward PE is the result of "revenue and profit both missing estimates plus a post-earnings sell-off," not a signal of long-term undervaluation. What the market is questioning right now isn't SK hynix's HBM technology leadership — it's whether analyst expectations themselves have started to outrun the actual pace of capacity ramp across the whole supply chain.

5. Peer group scan: the AI memory supercycle

Group definition: names tied to this cycle's AI-server-driven DRAM / HBM / NAND memory shortage. Covers the three big DRAM+HBM makers (SK hynix / Micron / Samsung) and the NAND side (WDC / SanDisk / Kioxia); the shared thesis is that AI data centers have opened a structural memory supply gap, with prices and margins spiking together.

6. What to watch next quarter (falsifiable)

  • Whether the HBM4 H2 ramp lands on the schedule management laid out — the only concrete timeline given on this quarter's call.
  • Whether Q3 revenue/operating profit re-clear Street estimates — this quarter was already "record yet a miss"; another miss in Q3 would shake confidence that the whole AI memory supply chain's growth expectations can actually be delivered.
  • Whether the company discloses a core net income/EPS figure excluding the one-off gain next quarter, so the market can cleanly judge the core profitability trend.

7. The bull and bear case

The bull case: SK hynix is the clear HBM leader, HBM4 has already started mass shipment with roughly 10 long-term customer agreements locked in, and the balance sheet is extremely healthy with ₩69.4T of net cash. After the sell-off, forward PE of 4.5x and a PEG of 0.06 are the lowest among the three major memory makers — if the H2 HBM4 ramp goes smoothly, this quarter's miss could turn out to be short-term noise.

The bear case: both revenue and operating profit missed estimates, which means even the company with the most secure technology position in this supercycle couldn't keep pace with the expectations the market itself created — that's not a sign demand is fading, but that capacity ramp may be slower than the narrative assumes. The headline net income figure is inflated by a ₩63.3T one-off gain; strip it out, and the real baseline for judging the core business is far more conservative than the reported number. The real disagreement is whether this miss is short-term ramp noise, or the first sign that supercycle expectations have started to outrun reality.
