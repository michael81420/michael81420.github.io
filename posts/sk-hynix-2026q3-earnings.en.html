SK hynix．2026 Q3 Earnings, Unpacked

HBM4 ramps and operating profit sets a record; net income and investment effects require separation.
2026-07-01 ~ 2026-09-30Released:2026-10-282026-10-31Draft complete · Unpublished

TL;DR

  • Revenue was approximately $73.873B (KRW104.6155T), up 32% QoQ; operating profit was approximately $58.845B (KRW83.3338T), up 38%.
  • Reported operating margin was 80%, above roughly 76% in Q2. Net income was KRW39.7756T; net and operating profit are different trends.
  • HBM4 shipments ramped. Management expects Q4 volume and revenue to exceed HBM3E; this remains a forecast.
  • LTAs with 14 customers cover over 50% of 2027–28 capacity; market-linked pricing does not guarantee fixed profit.

Segments

Server DDR5 and HBM4 drove DRAM; 321-layer TLC eSSD supported NAND. The release does not fully disclose product revenue, gross margin, core EPS or quarterly FCF; no other quarter is substituted.

Guidance

Management expects Q4 HBM4 shipment volume and revenue to exceed HBM3E, with M15X at maximum capacity by year-end. 2027 investment should rise substantially, but no numerical revenue/EPS or capex range was provided.

Prior watch items revisited

  • HBM4 shipments ramped, supporting the second-half expansion watch.
  • Operating profit set a record, but a comparable consensus check was not completed; record profit is not automatically a beat.
  • The official release still lacks core EPS excluding investment effects.
