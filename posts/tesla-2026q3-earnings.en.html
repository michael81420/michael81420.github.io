Tesla．2026 Q3 Earnings, Unpacked ｜🧾

Of last quarter's watch items, two hit and one missed: storage gross margin rebounded from 20.4% to 33.1% and auto margin ex-credits rose to 18.1%, but capex of $4.73B is far below the path needed to reach >$25B.
Revenue of $28.05B matched consensus, −0.2% YoY; operating margin 2.6% (5.8% a year ago); non-GAAP EPS of $0.48 beat by $0.03 but only with stock comp added back. IR shareholder letter cross-checked against Futu. Not investment advice.
Source: Tesla SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1, Q3 2026 Update Period: 2026-07-01 – 2026-09-30 Reported: 2026-10-21 after close Compiled: 2026-10-22

TL;DR · What actually mattered this quarter

📈 The margin rebound is real, and it came from all three segments at once. Total gross margin 19.6% (Q2 16.8%, a year ago 18.0%); storage 20.4% → 33.1%, confirming last quarter's claim that the cell warranty charge was one-time; auto ex-credits 16.3% → 18.1% (+270bp YoY); services 15.9%, another record. Gross profit $5.49B, +8.7% YoY.

🧾 But gross profit did not reach the bottom line. Revenue −0.2% YoY, opex $4.78B, +39% YoY (R&D $2.76B, +69%; stock comp $1.165B vs $663M a year ago, +76%). Operating income $718M, −56% YoY, operating margin 2.6% (5.8% a year ago). Non-GAAP EPS of $0.48 beat the $0.45 consensus by $0.03, but of $1,697M non-GAAP net income, $982M after-tax stock comp is added back (about $0.28 per share, 58%). GAAP net income was $787M (EPS $0.22); stripping out the bitcoin, SpaceX and tax items leaves about $715M (about $0.20 per share, derived).

💸 FCF turned positive at +$1.53B, but capex trails its path and cash flow includes working-capital release. Capex $4.73B (Q2 $5.79B, −18% QoQ), $13.01B year to date. Last quarter we calculated H2 needed about $8.35B per quarter to reach >$25B; Q3 fell $3.6B short, so Q4 implies about $12B (+153% QoQ). Of $6.26B operating cash flow, the Futu cash flow statement shows +$1.26B from "other working capital changes" (Q2: +$0.23B).

★ Also: deliveries 486,532 (+1.3% QoQ, −2.1% YoY), inventory 11 days (Q2 15). The company calls its passenger-car backlog the largest in four years and says "battery availability remains a limiting factor." Market reaction: not available — results came after the close on 10/21, Futu has no 10/22 session yet, and press reports on the after-hours direction contradict each other.

1. Net income: how much of the non-GAAP beat is stock comp

This site looks at core earnings, not headlines, and Tesla's non-GAAP has one feature to know: it adds back stock comp in full. SBC was $1.165B this quarter against $663M a year ago, +76%. So "non-GAAP EPS only 4% lower YoY ($0.50 → $0.48)" looks mild, while real people and R&D cost is eating the core business. On a GAAP basis it is $0.22 vs $0.39, −44% YoY.

Three items excluded from non-GAAP (IR reconciliation, after tax): unrealized digital-asset (bitcoin) gain $224M (deducted), unrealized loss on the SpaceX stake $108M (added back), and certain tax items $44M (added back). The digital-asset balance is $962M (Q2 $674M). Last quarter SpaceX contributed a $763M gain; this quarter the same line is a loss — GAAP net income will swing with the SpaceX share price, as we flagged last quarter.

★ Futu's income statement matches IR line by line. Small differences exist for Q2 (GAAP net income IR $1,114M / Futu $1,116M; FCF IR −$1,092M / Futu −$1,099M); IR is used throughout.

2. Core numbers: this quarter vs last quarter vs a year ago vs expectations

⚠️ The opex YoY base differs between sources: IR shows $3,430M a year ago → +39% (Electrek agrees); Futu shows +49.6% (implying a lower base). This post uses IR. The consensus figures for revenue ($28.06B) and EPS ($0.45) come from CNBC / Electrek (secondary); consensus for gross margin and operating margin could not be found, so a margin beat or miss cannot be judged.

3. Segments: three margin lines all moved up

① Storage: margin is back, revenue is not. Gross profit about $954M (33.1%), but revenue is $2.88B, −15.6% YoY (the company says partly due to tariff-related customer refunds). Deployments were 13.7 GWh (Q2 13.5, a year ago 12.5). Revenue ÷ GWh gives roughly $210M per GWh against $273M a year ago (derived): volume up, price per GWh down. Electrek cites an expected deployment of about 15.9 GWh (secondary), so volume fell short of that.

② Auto: margin up, revenue down. Auto revenue $20.56B, −3.0% YoY; GAAP auto margin 18.5%, 18.1% ex-credits (credits add only about 0.4pp). Last quarter's post set "16.3% ex-credits" as the new base; this quarter shows it was not a ceiling.

③ Services: revenue flat, gross profit keeps building. Revenue $4.61B (+32.5% YoY but only +0.5% QoQ), gross profit $734M (+100% YoY, +$86M QoQ). Active FSD subscriptions are 1.74M (Q2 1.48M, +17.6% QoQ; +67% YoY), yet services revenue barely moved QoQ — the subscription growth is not visible in revenue. A third-party note says every new car comes with a free month (unverified); if true, the count includes trials.

Revenue mix: auto 73.3%, storage 10.3%, services 16.4% (a year ago 75.5% / 12.2% / 12.4%). Gross-profit mix: auto 69.3%, storage 17.4%, services 13.4%.

4. Regulatory credits: still falling, but not below $100M

Auto regulatory credit revenue was $113M, −73% YoY (a year ago $417M) and −23% QoQ (Q2 $146M). Last quarter's watch item "does it fall below $100M" was not triggered, but the decline has not slowed: $417M to $113M in a year. The contribution to auto gross margin is down to about 0.4pp (18.5% vs 18.1%), so credits matter less and less for EPS, and future margin moves will be set almost entirely by manufacturing and pricing — which is actually good for reading the core business.

5. Capex and cash flow: behind the path, with working capital helping FCF

Quality of the $1.53B FCF: operating cash flow of $6.26B is flat on a year ago ($6.24B), but the Futu cash flow statement shows +$1.26B in "other working capital changes" (Q2 +$0.23B); deliveries of 486,532 exceeded production of 464,391 and inventory fell to 11 days. Excluding that line, FCF is about $0.28B (derived; the line is not a complete working-capital measure). Positive FCF does not mean capex pressure is gone — it means $1.06B less was spent this quarter.

⚠️ Guidance text vs execution: the Outlook section of the letter quantifies no capex, saying only that SI compute, solar, battery-material and semiconductor capacity build-outs are "underway." A third-party Shacknews live note says "revising capex guidance to $20 billion," which contradicts Electrek's statement that >$25B was not updated; that note is not a transcript and its share price differs between lines, so this post does not use it. The falsifiable test is Q4: does actual capex approach $12B?

8. Valuation

📐 Forward PE 389.6x (Futu), trailing 357.8x, one-year PE percentile 66.4% (after Q2: 284.7x / 28%). Forward PS 13.2x, PS percentile 40.8%. Close $386.47 (10/21, before results), +29.5% from the post-Q2 low of $298.32; the percentile has gone from 28% back to 66%. ⚠️ Futu's forward PE is above trailing, meaning its next-year EPS is lower than trailing four-quarter GAAP EPS; use it only as a relative position.

Cycle severity (invest-playbook): autos are a strong cycle, and policy (the tax credit that expired 2025/9/30, regulatory credits) moves demand and profit sharply. Gross margin swung 21.1% (Q1) → 16.8% → 19.6% in three quarters, so one quarter of rebound is not a trend. The share price still trades on AI/robotics narrative, not the auto cycle.

Analysts: 25 covering, average target $430.7 (high $550 / low $220), buy / hold / sell 56% / 40% / 4% (Q2: 24, $405.8, 41.7% / 50% / 8.3%) — buy share up 14pp, sells halved, target +6%.

9. Risks and the other side

Good numbers, but…

① Gross margin looks good while operating income is −56%. Gross profit is +$440M YoY while opex is +$1.35B (+39%); revenue has been flat for a year (Q3'25 $28.10B → Q3'26 $28.05B). If R&D (+69%) keeps growing at this pace while revenue stands still, there is no room for operating margin to improve.

② The EPS beat is stock comp added back, not core outperformance: a $0.03 beat against $0.28 of SBC add-back. On GAAP it is $0.22 vs $0.39.

③ Positive FCF partly reflects working capital and delayed capex: if Q4 has to catch up to ~$12B, FCF turns negative again.

④ Storage is "volume up, price down" and below expected deployments: the margin rebound is the one-time charge dropping out, not stronger pricing.

The bull case: backlog is the largest in four years and inventory is 11 days, so demand is not the problem and battery supply is the limit; all three segment margins are up; Optimus line start, volume Semi production and Megafactory Texas (planned 50 GWh) are all in the IR letter.

★ Versus last quarter, the thesis moves from "earnings quality broke in three places" to "margins are repaired; the cost structure and the capex pace are the new questions." This was a repair quarter, not a reversal, and should not be written up as a turning point.
