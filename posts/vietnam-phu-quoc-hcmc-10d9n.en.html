[Day 1｜Fri 12/25] Clear immigration at SGN, sunset on the island

Apply for the e-visa at least a week ahead on the official Vietnam e-visa site (watch out for look-alike agent sites). Phu Quoc's 30-day visa exemption only covers arriving on the island directly from abroad; this trip enters at SGN, so it does not apply.

Taoyuan → Saigon → Phu Quoc (03:30–15:00)

  • Leave Taipei at 03:30, meet at Taoyuan T2 at 05:00, Vietnam Airlines VN571 departs 07:45.
  • The SGN connection is 3h35 and you must clear immigration (per Vietnam Airlines' 9/1 notice, the 13:00 VN1827 became 14:00 VN1833): the second leg is domestic, so you cannot stay airside. Land at T2 10:25 → immigration → baggage claim → exit → free shuttle to T3 (10–15 min) → check in and drop bags again → security → depart 14:00.
  • Get the second boarding pass online beforehand and head straight for the immigration line; pack light so baggage claim doesn't hold you up.
  • Land at Phu Quoc International (PQC) 15:00; grab a Grab / Xanh SM at 15:40 after baggage claim, 20–30 min to the Duong Dong hotel. Seven people plus bags need two cars (or a 7-seater plus a 4-seater); the airport has a taxi queue.

Duong Dong beach / Sunset Sanato sunset (16:50–18:30)

  • 16:20 Check in, drop bags and head straight out — no time to change: late-December sunset is around 17:40, so today is for watching, not swimming.
  • Getting there: Xanh SM electric taxi, about 15 min and ~120k VND.
  • Why: Phu Quoc's west coast faces the sunset head-on. Sunset Sanato's beach is dotted with giant art installations — the island's most popular sunset spot.

Dinh Cau night market (18:30~)

  • Getting there: 5–10 min walk from the Duong Dong hotel.
  • Eat: grilled oysters (scallion oil or cheese), live seafood sold by weight and cooked to order, coconut ice cream. Ask the price per kilo and the cooking style before ordering.

[Day 2｜Sat 12/26] South island: the sea cable car, water park, Sunset Town

Grab / Xanh SM all day, seven people in two cars. Head straight to the water park in the morning and eat lunch inside the park (Bai Sao moved to Day 5, on the same run as Ho Quoc Pagoda). Getting back to Duong Dong from the south tip at night is the hard part — see the last section.

Hon Thom cable car + Aquatopia water park (09:30–16:30)

  • Getting there: Grab from Duong Dong to the An Thoi cable car station at 08:30, about 50 min and 300–400k VND per car.
  • Why: a roughly 8 km sea-crossing cable car, among the longest in the world — 15–20 min each way over fishing boats and islets. On Hon Thom, the Aquatopia water park opens around 10:00; eat lunch inside the park. Confirm cable car and park hours before you go.
  • Cable car and park are usually sold as a combo; buy ahead on Klook.
  • 16:30 Leave the park for the cable car back — the return line can be long in peak season, so leave early to make the 17:40 sunset.

Sunset Town + Kiss Bridge (Cau Hon) (17:00–21:30)

  • Getting there: about 10 min walk from the cable car station.
  • 17:00 A Mediterranean-style town; the two halves of Kiss Bridge almost touch over the sea — the best sunset frame.
  • 19:00 Dinner in town (walk).
  • 20:00 Kiss the Stars multimedia and fireworks show — confirm showtimes and tickets with the hotel or the official site.
  • 21:30 Grab back to Duong Dong, about 50 min. Everyone leaves the show at once, and getting two cars at the same time is hard: start booking at 21:00, or have the hotel pre-book a 7-seater. Fallback: skip the show and take the free Sun World shuttle back before 19:15.

[Day 3｜Sun 12/27] Island hopping: snorkeling, fishing, seafood on board

The Klook tour includes hotel pickup — enter your hotel name when booking, no van needed. Bring: high-SPF sunscreen, swimwear, seasickness pills (the speedboat is bumpy), a waterproof phone pouch, a change of clothes.

Three islands: Hon Mong Tay / Hon Gam Ghi / Hon May Rut (07:30–16:00)

  • 07:30 Hotel pickup; 08:30 board the speedboat at An Thoi port.
  • From 09:00: Hon May Rut has the prettiest beach, Hon Gam Ghi is the snorkeling stop, Hon Mong Tay is small and quiet. Fishing along the way, seafood lunch on the boat.
  • 16:00 Back to port; the tour van drops you at the hotel.

Seafood dinner (19:30)

  • 18:00 Back to the hotel to shower and rest.
  • 19:30 The Crab House or the Duong Dong seafood stalls. Getting there: 2 × Xanh SM, about 15 min.

[Day 4｜Mon 12/28] North island: Grand World and the Vin parks

16-seat van for the full day. Taxis are hard to find in the north at night, and everyone leaves Grand World at once after the evening show — the van is the safe bet. Duong Dong to the north is 35–40 min.

Grand World (10:00–14:00)

  • Why: a Venice-style canal town with gondola rides, plus a teddy bear museum and large bamboo structures.
  • 12:30 Lunch inside the park (walk).

VinWonders or Vinpearl Safari (14:00–18:00)

  • Pick one — you can't do both in an afternoon. A free shuttle links the two (about 10 min).
  • VinWonders: Vietnam's biggest theme park, mostly roller coasters and water rides — for thrill seekers.
  • Safari: an open-range zoo you drive through; a gentler pace.

Dinner at Grand World + water puppets (18:00–21:00)

  • Take the free shuttle back to Grand World for dinner and the evening water puppet show.
  • 21:00 Van back to Duong Dong — the driver is waiting, which is exactly why you book the full day.

[Day 5｜Tue 12/29] Ho Quoc Pagoda, Bai Sao, pepper farm, massage and souvenirs

Half-day van (08:00–13:40); release the driver back in Duong Dong at lunch and walk everywhere in the afternoon. You leave the island tomorrow at noon — pack tonight.

Ho Quoc Pagoda (08:40–09:50)

  • Getting there: leave Duong Dong at 08:00, about 40 min.
  • Why: the island's largest temple, set against the hills and facing the sea — the Buddha and the ocean in one shot. Cover knees and shoulders.

Bai Sao beach (10:10–11:40)

  • Getting there: about 20 min by van from the pagoda.
  • Why: widely rated the island's most beautiful beach — fine, creamy sand and shallow water. Mornings are quieter and the light is best.

Coconut Tree Prison + pepper farm (12:00–13:00)

  • Getting there: about 20 min by van from Bai Sao. The prison is free.
  • Why: a Vietnam War POW prison with very graphic displays — be prepared. Stop at a pepper farm on the way back; Phu Quoc black pepper is the top souvenir and you can buy on site.
  • 13:00 Back to Duong Dong (about 40 min); 13:40 lunch and let the driver go.

Massage + souvenirs + packing (15:00~)

  • 15:00 Full-body massage, 60–90 min, about 400–600k VND — have the hotel book it for the whole group.
  • 17:00 Souvenir shopping in Duong Dong: fish sauce, black pepper, cashews, pearls (see Souvenirs).
  • 19:00 Dinner, then pack.

[Day 6｜Wed 12/30] Fly to Saigon, Nguyen Hue walking street

PQC → SGN is domestic; arriving 1.5 hours early is plenty. Book the 13:00 van pickup with the driver the day before.

Last half-day on Phu Quoc (08:00–15:35)

  • 09:00 Check out and leave bags at the desk. 09:30 Last swim at the beach or pool, or top up at Duong Dong market.
  • 12:00 Lunch near the hotel (don't wander far).
  • 13:00 16-seat van to PQC, about 30 min; 13:45 bag drop; 15:35 VN1832 departs.

SGN T3 → District 1 (16:45–18:30)

  • Land at SGN T3 16:45; a 16-seat van (9 people + all luggage) leaves 17:20 for the District 1 hotel. It's rush hour — allow 45–60 min.
  • The two flying in straight from Taiwan meet the group at the hotel; check in 18:30.

Nguyen Hue walking street + The Cafe Apartment (19:30~)

  • Getting there: walking distance from District 1.
  • Why: Nguyen Hue is the city's central pedestrian boulevard, with City Hall at one end. No. 42 is an old apartment block turned into a café building — one shop per flat — best seen from across the street.
  • 21:00 Saigon River at night or drinks at a rooftop bar.

[Day 7｜Thu 12/31] Classic city loop and New Year's Eve on Nguyen Hue

All on foot or short Grab rides today. Streets around Nguyen Hue close on New Year's Eve and crowds are huge — book rooftop tables early, and walking back after the countdown beats waiting for a car.

Breakfast + Independence Palace (08:30–11:00)

  • 08:30 Pho or banh mi.
  • 09:30 Walk 10 min to Independence Palace: the former South Vietnamese presidential palace whose gates a North Vietnamese tank crashed through in 1975; the basement war rooms are preserved as they were.

Cathedral → Central Post Office → Book Street (11:00–12:30)

  • All three sit on one square, 3 min apart. Notre-Dame Cathedral is under renovation — exterior only.
  • The French colonial post office has a vaulted hall and vintage maps — great photos, and you can mail postcards.
  • 12:30 Lunch near Book Street.

War Remnants Museum → Ben Thanh Market (14:00–18:00)

  • 14:00 War Remnants Museum (12 min walk / 5 min Grab): Vietnam War archives and photography; the Agent Orange section is heavy.
  • 16:00 Ben Thanh Market (15 min walk) — start bargaining at half the asking price.
  • 18:00 Back to the hotel to rest and change.

New Year's Eve: rooftop dinner + Nguyen Hue countdown (19:30~)

  • 19:30 Rooftop dinner: Chill Skybar / Saigon Saigon / Social Club. Expect minimum spends or set packages on NYE — book early for 9.
  • 23:00 Countdown and fireworks on Nguyen Hue, a short walk away.

16-seat van for the full day, hotel pickup 07:30. The two routes go in opposite directions — pick one.

Dinner in Cho Lon (19:00~)

  • Getting there: 2 × Grab, about 20 min, 120–180k VND per car.
  • Why: Saigon's Chinatown, around Thien Hau Temple and Binh Tay Market — Cantonese food, Teochew noodles and herbal shops side by side.

[Day 9｜Sat 1/2] Pink church, coconut coffee, massage and a farewell feast

Tan Dinh Church → coffee (09:00–12:00)

  • 09:00 The pink church in District 3: 2 × Grab, about 10 min, 60–80k VND per car. The whole façade is pink; morning light is best.
  • 10:30 Coconut coffee smoothie at Cong Caphe, or specialty pour-over at The Workshop.

Malls, massage, souvenirs (12:00–18:00)

  • 12:00 Lunch and shopping at Takashimaya / Vincom Center, right by Nguyen Hue.
  • 14:00 90-min full-body massage: Temple Leaf / Miu Miu Spa. Book the day before for 9 people.
  • 16:30 Souvenirs: G7 coffee, Trung Nguyen, dried durian, cashews, fish sauce. Buy in town — the airport charges about double.

Farewell seafood + settling up (18:30~)

  • 18:30 Final seafood dinner (2 × Grab).
  • 21:00 Back to the hotel to pack and split the bill.

[Day 10｜Sun 1/3] Heading home

The return flight leaves from SGN T2 (international), not T3 where you landed — tell the driver. Sunday afternoon traffic is heavy; allow 30–60 min.

Last shopping and the flight home (08:30–21:10)

  • 09:30 Check out and store bags; 10:00 Ben Thanh or a supermarket for last coffee souvenirs; 11:30 lunch.
  • 13:00 16-seat van to SGN T2; 13:45 check in, drop bags, VAT refund.
  • 16:50 VN570 departs, land at Taoyuan T2 21:10. The last Airport MRT train leaves A12 around 23:00; with a big group and luggage, a car is easier.

Packing list

Documents and bookings

  • Passport (6+ months validity), Vietnam e-visa, flight tickets, boat tickets, Klook vouchers
  • eSIM, cash (Vietnamese dong, VND), travel insurance
  • International driving permit (if you plan to ride or drive)
  • Power bank: carry-on only, never checked — airlines limit capacity and in-flight use, so check before you fly

Clothes and toiletries

  • Change of clothes, swimwear / cap, light jacket (planes and malls are cold), sneakers, flip-flops, umbrella
  • Sunscreen, seasickness pills, waterproof phone pouch
  • Toothbrush and toothpaste, face wash, shampoo, soap, razor
  • Plug adapter, charging cables
  • Board games (9 people, evenings)
